About Us

The Pinec Story

Who We Are

Pinec Hotel was founded with one simple belief: guests in Ekeoha deserve hotel service of international standard without travelling to the city. What started as a small lodge has grown into a full-service hotel with comfortable rooms, a busy restaurant and bar, and one of the largest event halls in the area.

Our mission is simple — every guest leaves rested, well-fed and planning their next visit.

Why Choose Pinec?

  • 24-hour power, water and security — guaranteed
  • Honest pricing, no hidden charges
  • Online booking & card payment accepted
  • Staff trained to hospitality standards
  • Walking distance to Ekeoha Market and churches
  • Only 40 minutes from Owerri city

Good to Know

Hotel Policies

Check-in & Check-out

  • Check-in: from 1:00 PM
  • Check-out: by 12:00 PM (noon)
  • Early check-in / late check-out: subject to availability, small fee may apply
  • Valid ID required at check-in

Payment & Cancellation

  • We accept cards (online), bank transfer and POS
  • Online bookings are confirmed instantly after payment
  • Free cancellation up to 48 hours before check-in
  • Cancellations within 48 hours: one-night charge
  • No-show: full booking amount charged

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept card payments / POS?

Yes. You can pay online with your card when booking, or pay at the front desk via our POS terminal. Bank transfer is also accepted.

Is there WiFi?

Free high-speed WiFi is available in all rooms, the restaurant and the event hall.

Is power constant?

Yes — we run 24 hours with generator and inverter backup. Your room never loses power.

Can I bring visitors?

Visitors are welcome in the restaurant and bar areas until 9pm. Overnight guests must be registered at the front desk.

Do you offer airport pickup?

Yes, pickup from Owerri Airport or town can be arranged in advance — just contact us on WhatsApp before your trip.
